Connection Canvas

The application name is Connection Canvas.

Connection Canvas is a mobile application for iPhone, iPad, and Android.

Purpose of Connection Canvas

Connection Canvas is a private scratch-pad worksheet. People use Connection Canvas to load or type sixteen word faces on a 4×4 grid, mark colors, rearrange cards, jot notes, and save History while they solve a puzzle in another app. Connection Canvas does not score play, does not provide answers or spoilers, and does not replace any third-party game.

Connection Canvas works fully on the device. Saving History is optional. Syncing History across devices is optional and off unless you turn it on.

Features of Connection Canvas

  • Load a dated board (faces only), samples, manual entry, or on-device photo OCR
  • Multi-color marks, rings, color swap, sort, shuffle, and reset
  • Rearrange cards; save History; notepad and category hypotheses
  • Optional Tip Jar thank-yous that never unlock features

How Connection Canvas uses Google Account data

Connection Canvas requests Google Account access only if you turn on Sync History with Google on Android and sign in. The only permission Connection Canvas asks for is hidden application data in your own Google Account (OAuth scope drive.appdata).

That storage holds your worksheet History (faces you loaded or typed, color marks, card order, notes) so you can resume on another Android device signed into the same account. Connection Canvas cannot list, open, or change your regular files, Gmail, Photos, or shared folders. The developer of Connection Canvas cannot open your application-data folder. You can turn sync off in Connection Canvas Settings or revoke access in your Google Account.

Read the Privacy Policy for Connection Canvas. Connection Canvas’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
