What Connection Canvas is

Connection Canvas is an unofficial assistive scratch-pad app for organizing your own guesses on a 4×4 Connections-style word grid. You load or type the sixteen faces, mark colors, rearrange cards, jot notes, and save History while you solve the puzzle elsewhere. Connection Canvas does not provide answers or spoilers.

Optional History sync uses your Apple ID (iCloud on iOS) or your Google account (Drive app data on Android). Those copies stay in your account. Sync is off unless you turn it on.
