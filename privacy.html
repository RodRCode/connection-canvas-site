Privacy Policy

Connection Canvas · Last updated: July 24, 2026

This policy describes how the iOS/iPadOS app Connection Canvas (“the App”) handles information. The App is an unofficial assistive tool and is not affiliated with The New York Times.

Summary

  • No ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the App as shipped.
  • Your puzzle work is stored on your device; optional private iCloud sync uses your Apple ID.
  • Camera and Photos are used only when you choose those features.
  • Optional tips are processed by Apple as In-App Purchases; tips do not unlock features.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information the App stores

Depending on how you use the App, it may store on your device (and, if you enable sync, in your private iCloud/CloudKit data):

  • History sessions — grid faces you loaded or entered, your color marks, card order, hypotheses text, notepad text, memory snapshots, timestamps, and optional puzzle date / source labels.
  • Settings — preferences such as appearance, Auto-save History, sort priority, high-contrast cards, and whether History iCloud sync is enabled (stored via system UserDefaults / app settings).
  • Private puzzle fingerprints (if that feature is enabled in your build) — a hash of a board’s faces linked to a date you already loaded, used only to suggest a match for later OCR/manual import on your account. This is not a public database.

iCloud

If you turn on Sync History with iCloud and are signed into iCloud on your device, History (and related private app data in the same store) may sync through Apple’s private CloudKit / iCloud for your Apple ID across your devices. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings. You can turn sync off in the App’s Settings (restart may be required) or manage iCloud for the App in iOS Settings.

Camera and Photos

  • Camera — only if you photograph a grid for on-device OCR import.
  • Photo Library — only if you pick an existing photo for OCR, or save a screenshot of your marked grid when you choose to save.

OCR runs on device. The App does not upload your photos to a developer-operated server.

Network

If you choose to load a dated board, the App may request board faces from a publicly available New York Times JSON URL. That request is made from your device. Category titles/solutions are stripped and not intended to be stored. On image-based puzzle days, card images may be downloaded; alt text is not used as card faces by default (it can spoil visual clues). You can avoid network board load by using manual entry, samples, or photo OCR instead.

No developer analytics endpoints are used by the App as described in this policy.

Purchases (Tip Jar)

The App may offer optional consumable tips (In-App Purchases). Payment is processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your full payment card number. Tips are voluntary thank-yous; they do not unlock features, remove limits, or change App functionality. Apple’s App Store and purchase privacy terms apply.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not provide personal information of children through the App.

Data retention and deletion

  • Delete History entries in the App, or delete the App to remove on-device data.
  • For iCloud-synced data, also remove the App’s iCloud data via device Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud, or by disabling sync and clearing History, as applicable.

Your choices

  • Decline Camera / Photos permissions; those features simply won’t run.
  • Keep iCloud History sync off for device-only storage.
  • Turn off Auto-save History in Settings if you prefer only manual Save.
  • Use manual entry only if you prefer not to fetch a board over the network.
  • Skip the Tip Jar entirely; the App works fully without tips.

Third parties

The New York Times is a third party unrelated to this App. Use of any NYT services or content is subject to their terms and policies. Apple provides the App Store, iOS, iCloud, and In-App Purchase processing; their terms and privacy policies apply to those services.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly.
