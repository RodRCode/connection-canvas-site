Connection Canvas · iPhone & iPad

Quick help

  • Mark cards — Tap a color, then tap cards. Double-tap a card to clear its marks (with confirmation if marks remain).
  • Rearrange — Long-press a card, then drag. Tap Done or outside the grid when finished.
  • Load a board — Use the + / Load menu, or tap the date in the title bar. Browse month/year, then tap a day to load. Manual entry and photo OCR are also available.
  • History — With Auto-save History on (Settings, default), work is saved as you go. You can always use the toolbar Save button. Open History (clock) to reload a session.
  • Reset layout — Counterclockwise arrow returns cards to original seats and keeps your colors.
  • Share — Share or save a square image of your marked grid.

iCloud History

Settings → Sync History with iCloud. When on, History can sync across devices signed into the same Apple ID. Turn it off to keep History only on that device. Fully quit and reopen the app after changing this setting.

Board load failed

Network or service changes can block optional board fetch. Use Manual entry, a sample, or Photo OCR (crop to the 4×4 if possible).
