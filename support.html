Connection Canvas · iPhone & iPad · Last updated: July 24, 2026

Quick help

  • Open a day — Tap the date title at the top (calendar / History sheet). Use Previous / Today / Next, or pick a day. Purple rings mark days with saved work.
  • Swipe days — On the main board, swipe left/right to move to the next or previous puzzle day.
  • Mark cards — Tap a color chip, then tap cards. Multi-color marks show as stripes. Double-tap a card to clear its marks (with confirmation if marks remain).
  • Swap colors — Long-press a color chip, then tap another color; or use Swap under the palette to exchange two colors across the board (and matching hypotheses).
  • Rearrange — Long-press a card, keep holding, then drag (Home Screen style). In rearrange mode, use the row ↑/↓ arrows to move an entire row. Tap Done or outside the grid when finished.
  • Sort / shuffle / reset layout — Toolbar: sort by color priority, shuffle seats, or reset seats to the original load order (marks stay).
  • Memory — Snapshot slots 1 and 2 save/load a full grid + notes state.
  • History — With Auto-save History on (Settings, default), progress saves as you mark/reorder/note. You can still use the toolbar Save button. Reopen a day from the date sheet to resume.
  • Load options — In the date sheet: samples, manual 16-card entry, or photo OCR (crop to the 4×4 when possible).
  • Share — Share or save a square image of your marked grid.
  • Hypotheses & notepad — Hypotheses on iPhone via the list button; notepad under Settings (and on iPad via the notes control). Free-form notes; Scribble-friendly on iPad.
  • Tip Jar — Heart button (or Settings → Tip Jar). Optional tips only; nothing unlocks.

iCloud History

Settings → Sync History with iCloud. When on, History can sync across devices signed into the same Apple ID. Turn it off to keep History only on that device. Fully quit and reopen the app after changing this setting.

Board load failed

Network or service changes can block optional board fetch. Use Manual entry, a sample, or Photo OCR (crop to the 4×4 if possible).

Tips / In-App Purchases

Tips are optional thank-yous processed by Apple. They do not unlock features or change the app. If products don’t appear, check your network and App Store account; on TestFlight/sandbox, use a Sandbox Apple ID.
